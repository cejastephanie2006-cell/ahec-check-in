04 · Takeaways

What the data points to

How this was built. Responses were collected anonymously at six outreach events, then transcribed and coded by hand. Each response received one sentiment (positive, concern, mixed or neutral) and one or more theme codes. Spanish responses were translated, and quotes use the English translation.

Keep in mind. Each event asked a different mix of questions, so events aren't directly comparable. The symposium asked only about self-care, which is why it skews positive, and as a community event not every answer there came from a high school student. Quotes are limited to positive and encouraging responses to protect the people who shared something harder.
